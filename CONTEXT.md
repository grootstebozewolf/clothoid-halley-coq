# clothoid-halley-coq

A Halley solver for the clothoid \(G^1\) chord-length residual, with Coq identities, four language implementations, and a Zenodo-archived public record.

## Language

**Claim**:
A statement a reader of the README, paper, `CITATION.cff`, `LICENSE`, or the GitHub/Zenodo record would take as fact.
_Avoid_: assertion, marketing, note

**Green**:
The mint gate for a version: every numeric claim and proof of this work holds, and every other public sentence has been rewritten to match the audit. The archived PDF is the paper that was audited, not an earlier compile.
_Avoid_: ready, done, shippable

**Numeric claim**:
A measured or proved quantity of this snapshot: corpus size, this solver’s wall-clock and iteration counts, Coq admitted-free.
_Avoid_: statistic, result

**Word claim**:
A public sentence about other work, a product integration, a DOI pointer, a page count, or a date — rewritable from sources without new measurement.
_Avoid_: editorial, metadata
